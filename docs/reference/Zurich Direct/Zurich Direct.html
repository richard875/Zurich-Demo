Life cover and income protection offer financial security in different ways. Understanding their benefits helps you decide if you need one or both.

  1. Life Cover Insurance: Pays a lump sum to your loved ones if you pass away or are diagnosed with a terminal illness. It can cover debts, living costs, and education. Premiums depend on your age, lifestyle, job, and cover amount.
  2. Income Protection Insurance: Replaces up to 70% of your income if you can’t work due to illness or injury, helping cover everyday expenses—even for non-work-related conditions.
  3. Why Consider Both? Together, these policies provide broader protection—especially during major life changes like marriage or starting a family. Consider your financial responsibilities and how each policy could support your loved ones if times get tough.
  4. Anton’s Story: Anton, a 45-year-old engineer, had both covers. After an accident left him unable to work for 18 months, income protection paid 70% of his salary, keeping his family secure.
